01 / ABOUT

A musician built
for the stage.

Singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist and live performer.

Dan Brekke blends country, blues, rock and original music into a live show built around musicianship, personality and connection with the crowd.

Based in the Midwest, Dan has built his reputation through festivals, regional shows and live performances that bring together strong musicianship, original songwriting and a high-energy stage presence.
